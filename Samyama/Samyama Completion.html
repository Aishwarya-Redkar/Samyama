Namaskaram,

As you completed the Samyama program, to support you further, there are a few support systems being offered:

Samyama Satsangs:

Day long satsangs offered in select cities, which provide an opportunity to go through guided samyama and breathwatching practices. It is an invaluable opportunity to refine and deepen the practices.

Satsang details are available here for few cities, local volunteers will be informing you of the dates in the following weeks via email.

Samyama Sadhana program:

To help deepen and establish the practices, Sadhguru has specifically designed this program for those initiated into Samyama and breathwatching.

It is a 5 day residential program and is offered both in Isha Yoga Center(IYC), Coimbatore, as well as Isha Institute of Inner Sciences, Tennessee.

Support:

For queries regarding the practice please email at samyama@ishafoundation.org
