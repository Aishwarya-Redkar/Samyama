Check-in Process

Step 1: Check in pass: Please collect your
  • Samyama Checklist
  • Samyama Check - In Pass
  • Legal Consent form
which will be issued from 5:30am onwards. You will be allocated a time slot for check in and go through the luggage screening in Adiyogi Alayam and to enter the hall and to receive your bedding.

Step 2: Medical Screening: Ensure to have your medical kit while you are visiting the medical screening counter. Once you complete your medical screening, ensure the Samyama Checklist is stamped by the Medical Screening volunteer. (Medical kits will be available for purchase at a nominal price. Please ensure you have sufficient cash in INR. )


Step 3: Payment for Bedding:

  • Proceed to this counter (even if you are bringing your own bedding).
  • Volunteers will stamp the Samyama CheckList.
  • Bedding will be provided inside the hall.
  • For Rental Bed Booking, you will receive communication shortly.
Note : Participants (NRIs and overseas) who have paid the program fees can skip this step, as bedding is included in the program fees.

Step 4: Clothing, etc.: Complete any pending purchases for the program, ie. Samyama clothing, medication, toiletries, etc. Skip this step if you already have the items you need.

Step 5: Taxi Booking:
A detailed email on booking the cab/shuttle has already been shared. Please refer to the email with the subject: “Shuttle / Cab Facility for Upcoming Samyama Program".

Step 6: Valuables Deposit: Please deposit your valuables such as mobile phones, laptop, iPad, camera (It's best not to carry electronic gadgets).

Ensure to complete any purchases before submitting the valuables. Alternatively, you can keep some cash on you to complete purchases if any. After submitting the valuables you won't be able to access them.

Step 7: Check In: You will receive your Samyama participant card

Step 8: Luggage Screening: Participants’ luggage will be checked at the hall entrance. Before proceeding to this step, please ensure you do not have any unwanted items (electronic gadgets, mirrors, razor, watches, snacks, books, etc.), else you may be asked to go back to the Valuables Counter to deposit them.

Step 9: Hall Entry According to the time slot given on the check sheet you can enter the hall for receiving the bedding or place your own bedding and then you can leave the hall.

Note: For poornanga, further information regarding bedding and the medical kit will be sent separately.
