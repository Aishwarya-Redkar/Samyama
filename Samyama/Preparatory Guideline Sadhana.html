Namaskaram,

Below are the guidelines for Sadhana while preparing for Samyama.

Practices

The following practices must be done for a minimum of 60 days as part of the preparation before the Samyama program:

(In the video, Sadhguru mentioned 40 days; it has been changed to 60 days now.)

Surya Namaskar / Surya Kriya / Surya Shakti and Yogasanas

Alternate between:

  • Surya Namaskar / Surya Shakti (min. 12 cycles), or Surya Kriya (min. 3 cycles)
  • And Yogasanas
  • On Sundays or rest days, you may do both.

For example:

  • On Monday, Wednesday, and Friday, practice Yogasanas.
  • On Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday – practice Surya Kriya.
  • Best to practice both daily.
  • If you haven’t learnt Yogasanas or Surya Kriya, learn them at least 60 days before the program.

Shakti Chalana Kriya & Shambhavi

  • Practice at least once a day.
  • Best to do both kriyas, or at least Shakti Chalana Kriya twice a day.
  • Shoonya: twice a day.
  • If you haven’t done Shoonya Intensive or Bhava Spandana, attend the upcoming programs before Samyama.
  • Shoonya can be completed anytime before Samyama. Practicing it well in advance helps establish the Shoonya and Shakti Chalana Kriya practice, but completing it just before the program is also fine.

Sukha Kriya

  • Make sure to practice for at least one hour every day.
  • It is best to do Sukha Kriya sitting in Ardha Siddhasana, during the sandhya kalas—20 minutes before and after sunrise, noon, and sunset.
  • Ideally, it is done in one continuous stretch of one hour.
  • If that is not possible, ensure a minimum of 20 minutes or more in each sitting.

Ardha Siddhasana

  • Sit in Ardha Siddhasana whenever possible.

(In the video, Sadhguru mentioned Swastikasana; it has been changed to Ardha Siddhasana.)

  • You should be able to sit in Ardha Siddhasana for 1.5 hours at a stretch.
  • Begin by sitting in Ardha Siddhasana for shorter durations.
  • Use a cushion for support in the beginning, allowing the body to be at ease.
  • As you become more comfortable, you can remove the cushion support.
  • Coming out: Stretch the leg, pause for 2–3 minutes, then rise slowly.

Supporting practices:

  • Patangasana (Butterfly)
  • Shishupalasana (Rock the Baby)

These practices prepare your system for the Samyama program. The more you practice, the better—sufficient preparation ensures your body supports you rather than becoming a barrier to higher possibilities.
