Samyama Program Instructions

Since there will be no communication of any kind with anyone during the program including with Sadhguru Ishangas and volunteers be it through speech, looks, smiles, eye contact, gestures, or notes. The program instructions are being shared with you now so that you can familiarize yourself with them and prepare accordingly. If these instructions are not followed or you break the silence in any way, you will be asked to leave the program.

Language

The program will be conducted in English. Tamil and Hindi Translation will be available only for those attending Samyama First Time. Your language skills must be sufficient to fully understand instructions and the program content.

Program Instruction & Guidelines

Medication & Health

Medication: Since you will be in silence and not able to communicate in any way and for any reason, make sure to bring the necessary medication in case you become sick. Please bring the following:

  • Pain-relieving ointment or balms (such as Moov or Bengay for knees, head, back, etc.)
  • Medications for cold, cough, headache, diarrhea, body pain, fever, menstrual pain, heartburn, constipation, etc.
  • Any regular medication you are on
  • Ladies: Must bring sanitary napkins in sufficient quantity

Meals & Rest

  • Do not skip any meal. You must always eat the minimum amount of food that is served on the plate.
  • As the program is intense in nature, it's important to take care of these aspects.
  • You must sleep every night.

Personal Don’ts

  • No mirrors and shaving
  • No watches
  • No cell phones
  • No smoking
  • No jewelry (unless it can firmly stay on the body. Rudraksha mala is fine.)
  • No sharing of toiletries or any other items, even with family members.

Dress Code

Participants are required to wear only white or orange-colored clothing. Please avoid off-white or creamy shades of white.

  • White kurta top / pyjama bottom set
  • White and Orange T-shirts
  • White pants (Orange pants are not allowed)
  • All orange tops must be purchased from Isha Shoppe.
  • If you bring your own white clothes, they should have no borders, logos, or designs.
  • Zip colors in black are fine.
  • Under garments can be of any color.
  • Cotton is best. All warm clothing must be white.
  • Clothes should be loose-fitting and of appropriate length.
  • Please bring 8 sets of clothes for the program.

You can buy clothes online at Isha Life India or at the Isha Yoga Center.

Bedding (Indian Participants)

  • You can bring your own bedding or request it on rent from the Yoga Center for 8 days.
  • If you are bringing your own bed below are the specifications,
    - White bedding set (6 feet x 2.5 feet and 2-inch thick)
    - Sufficent pillows and white sheets.
  • If you are renting bedding from the Yoga Center, bedding set will include bed, pillow and white sheets.
  • Further information on bedding rental will be communicated one week prior to the program.
  • Please bring extra white bed sheets or white warm clothing as it will be very cold during the night.

Bedding (Overseas Participants & Repeaters)

The bedding will be provided as the program fee includes the charges for bedding.

Hall

  • Meditation backrests will be available for sale from Isha Shoppe. Only if you really need it can you buy a backrest.
    Note : Meditation chair is not available for rent. No hard objects are allowed inside the hall. This includes water bottles, medicines, eyeglasses, etc. Seperate area will be provided. Please bring hard case for your eyeglasses.
  • Number tags must be worn at all times.
  • Do not leave the program hall unless instructed.
  • Smoking, alcohol, or intoxicants are strictly prohibited.
  • If you break the silence in any way, you will be asked to leave.

Mobile Phone Usage

Mobile communication will not be possible from check-in until the end of the program. Phones must be switched off and deposited during check-in.

For emergencies, contact 83000 83111.

Valuables

All expensive electronic items and valuables (phones, laptops, jewelry, etc.) must be deposited during check-in. Avoid bringing them as storage is limited.

Please ensure that any sharp objects, such as Swiss knives or nail cutters, are not packed in your luggage, as they will not be accepted at the valuables counter and will not be allowed inside the luggage area.

Security Check

  • After check-in, all luggage will be carefully screened to ensure that only items permitted during the program are brought inside.
  • This is because in silence outside food or certain personal items are not allowed to maintain the program ambience.
  • So please avoid bringing snacks, eatables, watches, mirrors, or books in your luggage.
  • Kindly note: Food items from the Isha Shoppe are not allowed during the program. To avoid inconvenience, it is strongly recommended not to purchase any food from the Shoppe, as these items will need to be removed at the security check.

Check-in

  • Samyama & Samyama Sadhana - 8 days (Dec 16–23, 2025): Check-in before 12 PM on Dec 16, 2025.
  • You may arrive 1 day early to complete formalities.
  • Bring your ePass (printout or soft copy) to enter.
  • Best time to check in: Before 9 AM for smooth entry.

Note: Latecomers after 12 PM on Day 1 will not be allowed.

Check-out

  • Please plan your departure after 6:00 PM on the last day of the program.
  • You may stay overnight and check out before 8:00 AM the following morning.
  • The Foundation reserves the right to accept or revoke your registration for the program at any time.
  • For any queries, please submit your request through support.ishafoundation.org.

We look forward to having you with us for the program.
